Cookie Policy

We keep cookies to a minimum. This page lists every cookie and browser storage item our website uses, what each one does and how long it lasts.

Last updated 4 November 20262 min readKamboh Technologies (Private) Limited

The short version

  • No advertising cookies and no cross-site tracking.
  • Google Analytics cookies are set only after you click “Accept”.
  • A few essential items remember your theme and your cookie choice.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website saves in your browser. Similar technologies, such as local storage and session storage, let a website remember information on your device. In this policy, “cookies” covers all of these.

Essential storage (always on)

These items are needed for the website to work as you expect. They do not track you and are never shared with anyone.

NameTypePurposeDuration
kt-themeLocal storageRemembers whether you chose light or dark modeUntil you clear your browser data
kt-consentLocal storageRemembers whether you accepted or rejected analytics cookies, so we do not ask again on every pageUntil you change it or clear your browser data
kt-prefillSession storageBriefly holds details from Ask Kamboh AI when you ask it to fill in the contact formDeleted as soon as the form is filled, or when you close the tab

Analytics cookies (only with your consent)

If you click “Accept”, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand how many people visit, which pages are useful and where we can improve. We use Google’s Consent Mode, so these cookies are not set until you accept, and we do not use Analytics for advertising.

NameProviderPurposeDuration
_gaGoogleDistinguishes one visitor from another2 years
_ga_<ID>GoogleKeeps track of your current visit (session)2 years

Analytics data is kept for no longer than 14 months. Google’s own use of this information is described in how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Third-party content

  • Google Maps (Contact page): the embedded office map is provided by Google, which may set its own cookies when the map loads. You can use the “Get directions” link instead if you prefer.
  • Google Fonts: our fonts are delivered by Google, which receives your IP address to send the font files. Google Fonts does not set cookies.
  • Links to WhatsApp, Instagram and GitHub: these services set their own cookies only if you open them.

Admin area

Our private admin area, used only by our team, stores a sign-in session in the browser through Firebase Authentication. Visitors to the public website never receive it.

How to change your choice

You can accept or reject analytics cookies at any time using “Cookie settings” in the footer of every page, or the button at the top of this page. If you withdraw consent, analytics stops immediately. To remove cookies that were already set, clear the cookies for kambohtechnologies.com in your browser settings.

Most browsers also let you block or delete cookies entirely. Blocking essential storage may mean the website forgets your theme or asks for your cookie choice again.

Changes and contact

If we add or change cookies, we will update this page and, where needed, ask for your consent again. For questions, email info@kambohtechnologies.com. For more about how we handle personal information, read our Privacy Policy.
