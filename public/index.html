About us

Technology With Purpose. Innovation Without Limits.

Kamboh Technologies (Private) Limited is a software engineering company based in Johar Town, Lahore, Pakistan. We design and build mobile apps, web platforms and custom business software for companies at home and abroad.

Our development process

From Bold Ideas to Powerful Products.

Five phases, each ending with something you can see and approve. You stay in control of scope and budget the whole way through.

01/ 05
  1. 01

    Discovery

    We learn how your business works, who your users are and what success looks like. You get a clear scope, priorities and an estimate before anything is built.

    • Workshops
    • Scope document
    • Estimate
  2. 02

    Strategy & Design

    We map user journeys, then design and prototype the key screens. You click through the product and give feedback before a line of code is written.

    • User flows
    • Clickable prototype
    • Design system
  3. 03

    Development

    We build in short sprints and share a working version at the end of each one, so progress is visible and you can change direction early, when it's cheap.

    • Sprint demos
    • Staging builds
    • Code reviews
  4. 04

    Quality Assurance

    Every release is tested across devices, browsers and edge cases, with automated checks on the parts of the product your business depends on most.

    • Device testing
    • Automated tests
    • Performance checks
  5. 05

    Launch & Evolution

    We handle deployment and app-store release, then monitor, support and improve the product as your users and business grow.

    • Deployment
    • Store release
    • Ongoing support
Why Kamboh

Engineering Excellence at Every Step.

How we work matters as much as what we build. These are the principles behind every project we take on.

We choose proven, well-supported tools such as Flutter, Next.js and PostgreSQL, so your product is easy to maintain and easy to hire for.

Features are prioritised by the value they bring to your business. We will tell you when something is not worth building yet.

Clear structure, code reviews and documentation mean another developer could open the codebase and understand it quickly.

We plan data models and infrastructure for the growth you expect, without over-building for traffic you don't have yet.

Interfaces are designed around real tasks and checked with real users, so people can use your product without a training manual.

You get regular demos, a shared project board and direct contact with the people building your product.
