What happens next

From first message to kickoff.

No long sales process. Four simple steps, and you decide at each one whether to continue.

  1. We read your inquirySomeone who builds software reviews it, not a sales script, and replies with any questions.
  2. A short discovery callWe talk through your goals, users, timeline and budget, and suggest the simplest way to get there.
  3. Scope and estimateYou receive a written scope with priorities, assumptions and an estimate you can compare.
  4. KickoffOnce you're happy, we set up the project board and start with discovery and design.
