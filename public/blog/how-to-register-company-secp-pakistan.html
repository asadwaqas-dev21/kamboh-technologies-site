How to Register a Private Limited Company with the SECP: Step-by-Step Guide

By the Kamboh Technologies team4 November 20267 min read

Registering your business as a private limited company gives it a separate legal identity, makes it easier to open business bank accounts and sign contracts, and builds trust with clients. In Pakistan, companies are registered with the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP) under the Companies Act, 2017. Here is how the process works.

Step 1: Choose the right company type

The two most common options for small and growing businesses are:

  • Private limited company: at least two members and two directors. Suitable for partners or founders starting together.
  • Single member company (SMC): one person owns the company, with a nominee named in case something happens to the owner.

Both give limited liability, which means the owners’ personal assets are generally protected from company debts.

Step 2: Check and reserve your company name

Your company name must be available and acceptable under SECP rules. Names that are too similar to existing companies, misleading or restricted are usually not approved. Prepare two or three options so you have alternatives, and reserve the approved name through the SECP’s online system.

Step 3: Prepare your documents and information

Before filing, gather:

  1. CNICs (or passports for foreigners) of all directors and members
  2. Contact details, addresses and occupations of directors and members
  3. The registered office address of the company
  4. The main business activity (the principal line of business)
  5. The share capital and how shares are divided between members

The company’s Memorandum of Association (what the company does) and Articles of Association (how it is run) are also prepared at this stage.

Step 4: File for incorporation online

The incorporation application, together with the memorandum, articles and required details, is submitted online to the SECP along with the prescribed fee. The SECP reviews the application and may ask for corrections.

Step 5: Receive your certificate of incorporation

Once approved, the SECP issues a certificate of incorporation with a Corporate Unique Identification Number (CUIN). Your company now legally exists. Certificates can be verified online through the SECP’s verification service, which is useful when clients or banks want to confirm your registration.

Step 6: Register for an NTN with the FBR

A newly incorporated company should register with the Federal Board of Revenue (FBR) for a National Tax Number (NTN). You will need the NTN to open a business bank account, issue proper invoices and file tax returns.

Step 7: Stay compliant every year

Registration is the start, not the end. Companies have ongoing duties, including:

  • Filing annual returns with the SECP
  • Keeping proper books of account and company records
  • Filing income tax returns with the FBR on time
  • Reporting changes such as new directors or a new registered address

Missing these filings can lead to penalties, so it helps to keep a simple compliance calendar.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Choosing a name that is too similar to an existing company
  • Using an incomplete registered office address
  • Describing the business activity too vaguely
  • Forgetting NTN registration and tax filing after incorporation
