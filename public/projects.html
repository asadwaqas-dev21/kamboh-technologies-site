Web platform

Business Management Platform

One dashboard for sales, stock and staff across several branches, so owners see the whole business without chasing reports.

The problem
Each branch keeps its own spreadsheets, so owners wait days for numbers and decisions are made on outdated data.
Key features
  • Live sales, stock and staff figures per branch
  • Low-stock alerts and transfer requests
  • Role-based access for owners, managers and staff
  • Exportable daily and monthly reports
Stack
  • Next.js
  • PostgreSQL
  • REST APIs

Mobile Commerce Application

A shopping app with catalogue search, cart, wallet and card payments, and live order tracking from checkout to doorstep.

The problem
Customers order by phone or chat, orders get lost, and there is no easy way to reorder or track a delivery.
Key features
  • Fast catalogue search with filters
  • Cart, wallet and card payments
  • Live order status with push notifications
  • One-tap reorder from order history
Stack

  • Dart
  • Firebase
