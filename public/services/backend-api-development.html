Overview

What we deliver.

A solid backend is what keeps your apps fast and your data correct. We design databases for integrity and reporting, and build versioned APIs that your apps, partners and other systems can rely on.

We also connect the outside services your business depends on, such as payment gateways and ERPs, with authentication, permissions, audit logs and monitoring built in.

Who it's for

  • Apps that need a reliable backend and database
  • Businesses connecting systems such as payments or ERPs
  • Teams whose current backend is slow or fragile
Problems we solve

Sound familiar?

These are the issues businesses most often bring to us, and how this service fixes them.

The problemApps are slow because the backend was not designed to scale.

How we solve itWe design the database and queries for performance from day one.

The problemSystems do not talk to each other, so staff re-enter data.

How we solve itAPIs and integrations move data automatically between your tools.

The problemNo one knows how the backend works or how to change it.

How we solve itVersioned, documented APIs with clear structure any developer can follow.
